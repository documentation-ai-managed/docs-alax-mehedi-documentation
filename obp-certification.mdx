## Introduction to OBP Certification

Obligation Based Plastic (OBP) certification is a regulatory requirement in Bangladesh that mandates plastic producers and importers to manage their plastic waste obligations. PCREPBD provides a streamlined platform for obtaining and maintaining OBP certification through automated compliance tracking and verification.

Our certification process ensures that businesses meet their plastic waste management responsibilities while maintaining transparency and accountability throughout the supply chain.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Eligibility Assessment" icon="search">
    Evaluate your eligibility for OBP certification based on your plastic production or import volume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Application Submission" icon="file-text">
    Submit detailed application with production data, waste management plans, and compliance history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document Verification" icon="check-circle">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Certification Issuance" icon="award">

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compliance Monitoring

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,Python">
  ````javascript
// Monitor OBP compliance
const complianceData = {
  producerId: 'PROD123',
  period: '2024-Q1',
  wasteCollected: 25000,
  targetObligation: 30000
};

const monitor = await fetch('/api/certification/obp/monitor', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: {
    'Authorization': `Bearer ${apiKey}`,
    'Content-Type': 'application/json'
  },
  body: JSON.stringify(complianceData)
});

const result = await monitor.json();
console.log(`Compliance Status: ${result.status}`);
````
  ````python
import requests

# Check OBP certification status
data = {
    "producer_id": "PROD123",
    "period": "2024-Q1",
    "waste_collected": 25000,
    "target_obligation": 30000
}

response = requests.post('/api/certification/obp/monitor', 
                        json=data, 
                        headers={'Authorization': f'Bearer {api_key}'})
result = response.json()
print(f"Compliance Status: {result['status']}")
````

## Renewal Requirements

<Callout kind="alert">OBP certifications must be renewed annually with updated compliance data.</Callout>

Renewal involves:
- Submitting annual waste management reports
- Demonstrating continued compliance with EPR obligations
- Paying renewal fees
- Updating contact and operational information
