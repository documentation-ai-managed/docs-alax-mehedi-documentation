## Understanding EPR in Bangladesh

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is a policy approach that makes producers responsible for the end-of-life management of their products. In Bangladesh, EPR regulations require plastic producers and importers to manage the waste generated by their products throughout their lifecycle.

PCREPBD simplifies EPR compliance by providing automated tracking, reporting, and verification tools that help businesses meet their obligations efficiently and transparently.

## Automated Compliance Tracking

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,Python">
  ````javascript
// Check EPR compliance status
const complianceCheck = await fetch('/api/compliance/epr/status', {
  method: 'GET',
  headers: {
    'Authorization': `Bearer ${apiKey}`,
    'Content-Type': 'application/json'
  }
});

const status = await complianceCheck.json();
console.log(`EPR Compliance: ${status.percentage}%`);
````
  ````python
import requests

# Get EPR compliance report
response = requests.get('/api/compliance/epr/status', 
                       headers={'Authorization': f'Bearer {api_key}'})
status = response.json()
print(f"EPR Compliance: {status['percentage']}%")
````
</CodeGroup>
